# Explain

Explain the concept the author asks about at the level they need (ask if unclear): intuition first, then precise definition, a small example from a *different* context than their paper, common pitfalls, and 2–3 verified references for further reading (or say none were verified).

Offer to add a short entry to `notes/ai/glossary.md` (create with the AI-generated header). Make clear this is for their understanding: they must write their own explanation in the manuscript and cite primary sources.
