# Style models

Put a few papers whose writing style you like here, as PDF, `.txt`, `.md` or `.tex`. Ideally they come from your field or your target journal. Three to five is plenty.

*The Style Coach* (`/cw:summon style-coach`) measures and describes their style and compares your manuscript with it. It gives hints on patterns, never sentences to copy.

These files are other people's work, so they are git-ignored. That way you don't publish them by accident.
